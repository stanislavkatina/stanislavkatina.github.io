Experience by development phase

Preclinical and translational research

I have long-standing experience designing and analysing controlled animal experiments and translational biomarker studies in neuroimmunology, neurodegeneration and tau pathology. This includes longitudinal designs, biomarkers, proteomics, microRNA and other high-dimensional data. I also developed an end-to-end reproducible workflow for a pharmaceutical preclinical animal study, from raw-data validation through analysis, visualization and reporting.

Phase I and Ib

My early-phase work includes first-in-human studies, single-ascending-dose clinical-pharmacology studies, dose escalation, active dose comparisons and longer-term follow-up. Therapeutic areas include oncology and cellular or cytokine-based immunotherapy, neuroimmunology and tau immunotherapy, metabolic disease, endocrinology and immunology. Responsibilities have included design strategy, simulations, protocol and SAP input, safety, PK/PD, immunogenicity, biomarker analysis and senior statistical review.

Phase I/II

I have supported combined dose-escalation and expansion studies in oncology, including cellular immunotherapy, antibody-drug conjugates, dendritic-cell immunotherapy and advanced solid tumors. The work has covered Bayesian and conventional design simulations, cohort and dose-escalation strategy, dose-escalation charters and meeting presentations, safety, efficacy, PK/PD, biomarkers and integrated decision support.

Phase II

My Phase II experience includes proof-of-concept and efficacy studies in oncology and neuroimmunology. I contributed to single-arm and controlled designs, biomarker-defined cohorts, longitudinal cognitive outcomes and a Bayesian adaptive proof-of-concept design for an acute immune-mediated neurological condition. Work has included protocol and SAP development, estimands, missing-data simulations, TLFs, analysis, interpretation and publication.

Phase II/III

I contributed statistical sections for synopses and protocols and performed statistical analyses for randomized active-controlled oncology studies across localized, non-metastatic and metastatic disease settings. The principal outcomes included progression, survival and safety.

Phase III

My Phase III portfolio includes global randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled studies in autoimmune and inflammatory disease, nephrology and dermatology; oncology studies; an acute-pain study; and active-controlled pediatric vaccine studies. My work includes statistical programming and review, SDTM and ADaM review, TLFs, pooled analyses, subgroup and longitudinal modelling, predictive modelling, senior statistical review and reproducible reporting.

Phase IV

As Lead Statistician for a cardiovascular Phase IV study at Charles University, I developed an end-to-end reproducible workflow covering data processing, validation, statistical analysis, visualization and publication-ready outputs. I performed and interpreted the analyses and contributed extensively to the scientific manuscript.

Real-world evidence and registries

My RWE experience includes prospective multicountry non-interventional studies in dermatology, allergology and immune-mediated disease, together with long-term work on a national orthopedic arthroplasty registry. Responsibilities include effectiveness and safety analyses, treatment patterns, quality of life, healthcare-resource use, longitudinal follow-up, data quality, implant usage and survival, annual reporting and interactive applications.

Responsibilities grouped by type of engagement

Statistical leadership in early-phase oncology

As Lead Statistician, I provided statistical leadership across study design, conduct, analysis and reporting. Responsibilities included protocol development and review, Bayesian and conventional design simulations, dose-escalation strategy, dose-escalation charters and meeting presentations, SAPs and TLF shells, review of data-management documentation, and development or review of SDTM and ADaM specifications. I reviewed and validated SAS programs and supported efficacy, safety, PK/PD, biomarker and exploratory analyses, DSURs/ASRs, CSRs, public trial-registration submissions and scientific publications.

Statistical programming and data science in global programs

I provide statistical-programming and data-science support across global Phase III and RWE programs. My work includes programming and review of efficacy and safety analyses, pooled and subgroup analyses, longitudinal modelling, and exploratory and predictive modelling. I extensively review SDTM and ADaM datasets and specifications, review define.xml, and program or review TLFs. I also review and validate SAS programs, reproduce selected analyses in R, and develop reproducible and interactive reporting solutions.

Lead programming for a prospective RWE study

As Lead Statistical Programmer, I am responsible for validation and independent double programming of TLFs and senior review of data-management and statistical documentation. I review SDTM and ADaM datasets, specifications and related study data, help ensure consistency across protocol, analysis requirements, data structures and outputs, and design and program an R Shiny application supporting interactive study review.

Senior review within a CRO

As Senior Statistician within a CRO, I provided senior statistical review across Phase I and Phase III studies. This included review of protocols, SAPs, TLF shells, statistical methods, programs and outputs, together with review of safety, efficacy, PK/PD, corrected-QT or immunogenicity results, as applicable.

Neurodegeneration and integrated biomarkers

I provided senior statistical and bioinformatics leadership across Phase I and II clinical, cognitive, language, functional, safety, immunogenicity and biomarker analyses. This included protocol and SAP input, TLF development and review, longitudinal and exploratory modelling, reproducible reporting, and end-to-end analysis of phosphorylated tau, neurofilament light, proteomics, microRNA and other complex biomarker data.
