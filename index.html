Who I Am

I am a Principal and Senior Principal-level biostatistician, statistical programming leader and quantitative scientist who brings together scientific strategy and hands-on delivery. My perspective has been shaped by 28+ years across clinical development, preclinical and translational research, real-world evidence, statistical methodology, software development and university teaching.
