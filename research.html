Research & Publications

Clinical evidence, neuroimmunology and quantitative biomedical science

My research connects statistical methodology with biomedical questions across clinical trials, neuroimmunology, neurodegeneration, biomarkers, omics, medical imaging and statistical shape analysis. I have contributed to more than 130 peer-reviewed publications.

The unifying principle across this work is the disciplined construction of evidence. Scientific design, measurement, data validation, modelling, computation, software, visualization and interpretation are strongest when they form one coherent system. This perspective—developed across my Doctor of Science thesis—connects my methodological research with clinical and biological applications rather than treating them as separate tracks.

Major research themes

Clinical and translational biostatistics

Study design, longitudinal analysis, survival outcomes, biomarkers, PK/PD, integrated evidence, missing data and publication-ready statistical reporting.

Neuroimmunology and neurodegeneration

Long-standing collaboration with an academic neuroimmunology research institute, including Alzheimer’s disease, tauopathies, animal models and translational biomarker studies.

Shape, image and signal analysis

Statistical shape analysis, computational neuroanatomy, 2D/3D morphometrics, imaging, functional data, EEG/ECG and visualization of complex biological structures.

From experimental design to publication

I design and analyse preclinical animal experiments and translational studies, including longitudinal and high-dimensional investigations. My contributions include methodological strategy, data validation, statistical modelling, visualization, interpretation, reproducible reporting and manuscript preparation.

In clinical research, I have written or reviewed statistical methods and results, contributed to study publications and worked with medical writers on public clinical-trial reporting.
